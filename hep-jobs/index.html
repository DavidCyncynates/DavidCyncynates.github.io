About the search and deadlines

What is included

Faculty searches and clearly labeled fellowship-hosting expressions of interest from the 2026–2027 application cycle. Explicit HEP and cosmology opportunities are distinguished from broad physics searches and adjacent astrophysics or gravity searches. A hosting offer is not a funded faculty vacancy.

How listings are checked

Institutional recruitment pages, department pages, national programmes and specialist job boards are used together. Listings link to their evidence and retain a last-checked date. Ambiguous dates, eligibility or cycle information remain in a separate review view. Regional gaps and inaccessible sources are retained for follow-up. A weekly refresh is scheduled for Mondays at 11:00 Europe/Rome.

How dates work

Hard deadlines and confirmed closures remove jobs from the default view. A passed priority or review-start date does not establish closure. Host expressions of interest and national funding deadlines are separate stages; a later national deadline cannot reopen a closed host call.

Colors refer to the next advertised local milestone, including review dates. Where a submission timezone is absent, the map retains a listing until the stated date has ended everywhere. Always check the advertisement for the exact cutoff.

Institution profiles

US labels use the official 2025 Carnegie research designations. International profiles describe institution type and do not claim equivalence to R1 or R2. Map coordinates identify the city or campus, not an application office.
