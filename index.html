Particle phenomenology at the intersection of dark matter, cosmology, and gravity.

I am a postdoctoral fellow in the High Energy, Cosmology and Astroparticle Physics group at ICTP. I work on physics beyond the Standard Model, with a focus on axions and other ultralight fields, dark-matter production and detection, and nonlinear dynamics in the early Universe.

01 · Research

Well-motivated targets for new physics.

My work connects theoretical structure and cosmological history to observable signatures in laboratory experiments, astrophysical systems, and the large-scale Universe.

I

Axions & ultralight fields

Coupled axion sectors, the string axiverse, QCD-axion dark matter, and baryogenesis driven by light fields.

II

Predictive dark-matter targets

Using early-Universe dynamics to identify concrete targets for fifth-force, direct-detection, collider, and astrophysical searches.

III

Nonlinear dynamics & cosmology

Resonance, fragmentation, oscillons, and other nonlinear phenomena that reshape the observable consequences of light fields.
